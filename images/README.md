## Tips
- **Shape:** the scale photos are cropped into hexagons, so keep the subject in the center.
- **Size:** about 1200 px wide is plenty. Very large files make the site slow.
- **File type:** if your photo is `.png` or `.jpeg`, rename it to `.jpg` or convert it. The name must match exactly.
